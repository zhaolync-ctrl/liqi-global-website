About LiQi

Your Custom Toy & Promotional Products Manufacturing Partner

LiQi is a professional manufacturer specializing in custom plastic toys, promotional gifts, keychains, souvenirs and corporate promotional products. We provide OEM and ODM manufacturing solutions for overseas brands, importers, wholesalers and distributors.

We operate two specialized factories — Quanzhou Liqi Plastic Products Co., Ltd. and Jinjiang LiQi Mould Co., Ltd. — covering mold development, mold production, plastic injection molding, pad printing, spray printing, assembly and packaging.

  • ✓One-stop service from concept and design to finished, packaged product
  • ✓In-house mold workshop for faster development and better control
  • ✓Flexible customization on shape, color, printing and packaging
  • ✓Experienced team serving brands and importers worldwide

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often from new partners.

What kind of products can LiQi make?

We manufacture custom plastic toys, figure toys, PVC products, candy toys, promotional bags, gifts and stationery. Most items are made to order according to your design, color and packaging requirements.

Do you support OEM and ODM projects?

Yes. We provide both OEM and ODM services — from your own design and artwork to full product development, mold making, production and packaging.

What is the typical production process?

Inquiry and quotation, sample development, mold making (when needed), injection molding, printing, assembly, quality inspection and final packaging. We keep you updated at each stage.

Do you make molds in-house?

Yes. Jinjiang LiQi Mould Co., Ltd. handles mold development and production, giving us better control over tooling quality and lead time.

How do I get a quote for my project?

Send us your product idea, drawings or a sample, along with quantity and packaging requirements. Contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp and our team will get back to you.

Where is your factory located?

Our facilities are in Jinjiang, Quanzhou, Fujian, China — Anping Industrial Area, Anhai Town. Partners are welcome to arrange a factory visit.
